    Your data

    Saved language and moments stay on this device. Photos and audio are not saved to your collection. Live activities send the photo, your note and responses to OpenAI through the app’s server. Recording stays on your device until you choose to transcribe it; transcription sends the audio to OpenAI. API requests use storage disabled where supported, but provider retention policies still apply.

    To install on iPhone: open in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use your browser’s install option.

    Prototype · AI can misread images and make mistakes. Level selection guides the activities; it is not a proficiency assessment.
